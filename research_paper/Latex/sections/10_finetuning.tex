\section{Fine-tuning Experiments}\label{sec:finetune}

All results in this section are \evlabel{validation} findings; no fine-tuning result is confirmed.

All fine-tuning starts from a trained SAE and runs 500 Adam steps (learning rate $10^{-5}$) with the sparsity
controller continued from the base coefficient; ReLU and JumpReLU must stay within $\pm$5\,\% of their base L0.
Truth is measured with probes fitted on original activations of GoT training groups B, which no training objective
sees.

\paragraph{A circular objective, found and corrected.} A truth-aware objective that distilled a linear probe's logits
appeared to remove most of the truth gap. An audit found that the evaluation probe was the training teacher (same
rows, labels and weights), so the result was circular. In a corrected design (teacher fitted on groups A, probes on B,
random-teacher control) the truth-aware objective is worse than plain reconstruction fine-tuning on every seed
(TopK B-MLP gap 0.189 vs 0.107; random teacher 0.165).

\paragraph{Method A and output-KL at layer 6.} Table~\ref{tab:ft6} reports Method A (GoT reconstruction with OWT
replay) and the output-KL variant. Method A cuts GoT reconstruction error by 72--82\,\% and lowers the independent MLP
truth gap on 18 of 18 paired comparisons against both the base and an OWT-only control, but never by more than 2 sd,
the pre-registered criterion (\evlabel{preliminary}). Adding output KL lowers OWT spliced KL (0.115$\to$0.102) and GoT
statement KL (0.110$\to$0.038) for TopK, meeting its behavioural criterion without changing the truth gap; for ReLU and
JumpReLU the KL term pushes L0 5--11\,\% above the base, so their gains are not creditable. A KL-weight ablation found
no truth trade-off to optimise (\evlabel{inconclusive}).

\subsection{Multi-layer improvement experiment (IMP-L)}\label{sec:impl}
To test whether the layer-6 findings generalise, we repeated both methods at layers 3, 8 and 9 with an OWT-only
control (500 steps of continued training on OWT alone), for all three architectures and seeds: 81 new fine-tunes,
evaluated on validation data only. Configuration, criteria and guards were fixed and hashed before any run
(\texttt{results/geometry\_of\_truth/multilayer\_finetune/preregistration.json}); they are those of layer 6. A
criterion counts as met (``consistent'') when the arm is better on all three seeds and the mean paired difference
exceeds twice its standard deviation. Guards require OWT L0 within $\pm$5\,\% of the base SAE, OWT explained variance
no more than 0.005 below the base (output-KL: 0.02 below Method A), spliced $\Delta$CE at most 1.10$\times$ the base, and
no loss of cities truth preference beyond 0.018. All arms are \evlabel{validation} findings; the 95\,\% intervals over
three seeds are wide by construction ($t_{0.975,2}=4.30$). Table~\ref{tab:impl_summary} gives the pre-registered
classifications and Appendix Tables~\ref{tab:impl3}--\ref{tab:impl9} all paired differences with 95\,\% intervals; Figure~\ref{fig:impl} shows the
per-arm means.

\paragraph{Method A.} Method A cut GoT geographic reconstruction error by 61--85\,\% in every cell, passed every guard,
and lowered the independent truth gap (B-group MLP probe fitted on original activations) relative to the base SAE on
all three seeds in all nine layer--architecture cells (mean reduction 4--47\,\%). The OWT-only control changed the gap by
at most 0.021 in either direction, so the reduction is attributable to the GoT data. The pre-registered criterion
(consistent against both base and control) was met in four of nine cells: TopK at layer 3, ReLU at layer 8, and ReLU
and JumpReLU at layer 9 (\evlabel{preliminary improvement}). In the other five the direction was the same but seed
variation exceeded the 2\,sd rule (\evlabel{no improvement} under the criterion). Layer 3 counts as truth-informative
under the pre-registered rule (original B-MLP AUROC 0.815), although the original logistic probe there reaches only 0.60.

\paragraph{A frozen-probe gain that re-trained readers do not show.} A metric reported but not pre-registered as a
criterion qualifies this result: the M2-style re-trained logistic gap on GoT geographic statements, and on RepE-LM
persona prompts, did not change consistently under Method A relative to the base in any cell (no cell meets the 2\,sd
rule; all 95\,\% intervals include zero). Against the OWT-only control, one cell (TopK, layer 8, geographic) is
consistently lower. In the four cells where the frozen-probe criterion was met, the re-trained geographic gap changed
by $-$0.005 to $+$0.010 (three of four upward; ReLU at layer 9 upward on all three seeds). Method A therefore mostly restores what the frozen original-activation probe reads, without measurably restoring the information a
re-fitted reader can recover. This mirrors the M2 finding that frozen-probe gaps are dominated by probe fragility
(\S\ref{sec:info}), and it means the Method A truth improvements should not be read as information recovery
(\evlabel{descriptive}).

\paragraph{Output-KL.} Adding the output-KL term lowered OWT spliced KL by 8--18\,\% and GoT statement KL substantially
relative to Method A, consistently on every seed in every cell, and lowered spliced $\Delta$CE by 4--19\,\%. But for ReLU
and JumpReLU it also raised L0: by 3.5\,\% and 4.6\,\% at layer 3 (within the guard) and by 5.3--8.1\,\% at layers 8 and 9
(outside it). The behavioural criterion is therefore met (\evlabel{preliminary improvement}) for all three
architectures at layer 3 and for TopK, whose L0 is fixed by construction, at layers 8 and 9; the four ReLU and JumpReLU
cells at layers 8 and 9 are \evlabel{not creditable} and excluded from rankings. Output-KL never improved the truth gap
over Method A. It was consistently worse at layer 3 for ReLU and TopK (\evlabel{regression} under the 2\,sd rule, by
$+$0.012 and $+$0.027; the 95\,\% intervals just reach zero) and also worse on all
seeds at layer 9 for ReLU, a cell classified not creditable because of its L0 guard.

\paragraph{Summary.} Across three further layers the layer-6 pattern recurs: task-data fine-tuning reliably lowers task
reconstruction error and nudges the frozen truth gap down, and an output-KL term reliably improves behavioural
faithfulness, but neither improves truth information as measured by a re-trained reader. For ReLU and JumpReLU at
deeper layers the behavioural gain comes with a sparsity cost that the pre-registered $\pm$5\,\% guard does not allow.
