\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Sparse autoencoders.} Sparse dictionary learning on transformer activations yields many interpretable
latents \citep{bricken2023monosemanticity,cunningham2023sparse}. Later architectures fix the number of active latents
(TopK; \citealp{gao2024scaling}), apply that constraint over a batch \citep{bussmann2024batchtopk}, or learn per-latent
thresholds (JumpReLU; \citealp{rajamanoharan2024jumping}). Public suites exist for GPT-2 Small
\citep{bloom2024gpt2,saelens2024} and Gemma 2 \citep{gemmascope2024}; we use them as external references rather than
as objects of improvement.

\paragraph{Evaluating SAEs.} \citet{gao2024scaling} study how SAE quality scales with width and compute and argue that
reconstruction and sparsity should be complemented by downstream measures, including the change in model loss when the
reconstruction is spliced in. SAEBench \citep{karvonen2025saebench} evaluates SAEs on a broad suite of downstream
metrics and finds that reconstruction and sparsity proxies do not reliably predict practical usefulness, AxBench
\citep{wu2025axbench} finds that simple baselines outperform SAE-based methods for concept detection and steering, and
\citet{paulo2024automatic} automate the interpretation of SAE latents at scale. \citet{engels2024darkmatter} analyse the
structure of the reconstruction error, \citet{korznikov2026random} propose random-decoder sanity baselines, and
\citet{chaudhary2024evaluating} compare public GPT-2 SAEs with supervised subspaces. Our study is narrower than these
benchmarks, with two models and one task family per question, but it separates four faithfulness levels, pre-registers
each comparison and pairs every measure with a noise, PCA, synonym or random-subspace control.

\paragraph{Training for downstream faithfulness.} \citet{braun2024e2e} train SAEs end-to-end with a KL objective on the
output distribution, and \citet{karvonen2025kl} show that a short fine-tune recovers much of that benefit. Our
behaviour-targeted fine-tunes follow this idea; their contribution here is to measure what such an objective does to
the other three levels when sparsity is held fixed.

\paragraph{Causal analysis and probing.} Causal mediation analysis \citep{vig2020causal} and activation patching,
as in causal tracing of factual associations \citep{meng2022locating}, locate where a model carries information by
intervening on its activations; \citet{heimersheim2024patching} discuss how such patching results should be
interpreted. Attribution patching approximates these interventions \citep{syed2023attribution}, and sparse feature
circuits use SAE latents as the units of a causal graph \citep{marks2025sparse}. Our site sweep follows the patching
paradigm, and our decomposition asks a question that precedes SAE-based circuit work: how much of a located effect the
reconstruction retains. Because our patches keep the SAE error term, they measure that retention rather than mediation
through SAE nonlinearities. \citet{marks2023geometry} report linear truth structure using the Geometry of Truth
datasets, and \citet{zou2023representation} introduce the persona-contrast prompts from which our original task was
derived. Probe accuracy alone is an ambiguous measure of what a representation encodes
\citep{hewitt2019control,belinkov2021probing}; our separation of frozen from re-trained readers, with noise and PCA
references, addresses one form of this ambiguity.
